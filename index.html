What can I do with Stereo Markets?

Research stocks through technical and fundamental perspectives, manage a watchlist, track holdings and review portfolio allocation. The platform brings these workflows together; available market coverage depends on the underlying data sources.

Are the research scores predictions of profit?

No. Technical signal agreement describes the alignment of the model’s signals. Fundamental evidence quality describes the support and coverage behind a business assessment. Neither is a guaranteed outcome, a probability of profit or a promise of future returns.

Is this personalised financial advice?

Stereo currently provides information and research tools, not personalised investment advice. Consider your own objectives and risk tolerance, and consult an appropriately registered professional when you need advice.

Does Stereo execute trades?

No. Adding holdings or recording a sale updates your portfolio records. It does not place an order with a broker. Broker integrations are shown only when configured and available.
