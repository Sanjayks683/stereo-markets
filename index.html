Talk to Stereo
What can I do on the platform?

Research companies through technical and fundamental models, explore markets, screen curated coverage, compare companies, build watchlists, track holdings, read publisher news and arrange a research workspace. Existing notes, alerts, CSV imports and configured broker connections remain available.

Are scores predictions of profit?

No. Technical signal agreement describes alignment among technical signals. Fundamental evidence quality describes the support behind a business assessment. Snapshot check radars are separate, transparent research aids. None is a probability of profit or a guarantee.

Is the market data live?

Check each quote’s source, timestamp and delay label. Availability depends on the data source and exchange. We do not promise a real-time entitlement without a configured, verified data agreement. Public illustrations are labelled as sample data.

Does Stereo execute trades?

No. Adding a holding or recording a sale updates your portfolio records; it does not place a broker order. Confirm any actual order in your broker’s own application.

How do I get access?

Use Request access to contact us. No payment information or investment commitment is required to enquire.
