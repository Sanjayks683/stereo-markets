What is Stereo Markets?

A research and portfolio workspace that brings technical analysis, fundamental analysis, charts and holding context together. It helps you investigate a stock and keep track of your own research.

How are technical and fundamental analysis different?

The technical view examines price, momentum, trend and volume. The fundamental view examines the business and its financial statements, with valuation scenarios where sufficient evidence is available. They can disagree without either being a short-term price promise.

Does adding a holding place a trade?

No. Portfolio actions record and organise your holdings. They do not execute an order or move money through your broker.

Are the prices and analysis always live?

No. Availability and delays depend on the data source, exchange and connection. Check the timestamp and data status in the workspace before relying on a figure. The preview on this page uses illustrative data.

Does AI analysis guarantee a return?

No. Models can be wrong, historical results do not guarantee future outcomes, and an evidence score is not a probability of profit. Stereo Markets is not registered with SEBI as an investment adviser or research analyst.
